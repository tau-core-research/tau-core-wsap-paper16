# Data Notice

This repository contains a synthetic methodological demonstration only.

No real astronomical, cosmological, lensing, galaxy-rotation, or survey data
are analyzed in this package.  The compact files in `data/derived/` are
derived synthetic result artifacts from the WSAP v1.0 package:

- `results_v10_numeric.json`
- `v10_results_table.csv`
- `version_history.csv`

The manuscript explicitly does not claim physical detection, empirical
validation, dark-sector explanation, MOND/RAR replacement, or real-data
confirmation of Tau Core.
